# Open Rig brand

Open Rig is the full-stack agent harness for OpenCode: an operating-system
layer for agents, not merely a plugin collection.

**Hero:** The autonomous agent harness for OpenCode.
**Support:** Give your AI agent eyes, hands, memory, tools, and the ability to
operate your computer.

Use capability-first language: desktop control, browser access, repository
awareness, memory, maintenance, and resilient routing. Say **Open Rig** in
product-facing copy; OpenCode is the underlying runtime.

## Visual system

The Docked Frame mark combines an open structural frame with one connected
module. The frame represents the dependable harness around an agent; the
opening represents extensibility; and the docked module represents capabilities
connected through a deliberate interface. The silhouette may suggest an `O`,
but it should not be presented as a hidden-letter puzzle.

## Construction and spacing

The symbol uses a 32-by-32 unit grid, four-unit frame thickness, and square
corners. Keep at least four units of clear space around visible geometry; use
eight where space permits. Do not redraw with strokes because stroke alignment
changes at small sizes.

- Minimum screen size: 16 by 16 CSS pixels.
- Preferred interface sizes: 24, 32, or 48 pixels.
- Minimum horizontal lockup height: 32 pixels.
- Use the symbol alone for favicons, avatars, and narrow navigation.
- Keep the tagline outside the lockup as accessible live text.

The primary identity is one color. Use Rig Ink on Paper and Mist on Night.
Deep Teal and Signal Mint are supporting interface accents, not separate colors
for pieces of the primary mark.

## Misuse

Do not close the frame, detach or multiply the module, vary the frame thickness,
round individual corners, rotate, stretch, mirror, outline, add gradients or
effects, replace the `O` in the wordmark, or embed another project's logo. Use
plain `Open Rig` text in terminals rather than inventing an ASCII approximation.

The identity is an original design direction, not a trademark clearance. Run a
visual similarity review before treating it as a registered or exclusive mark.

The SVGs are self-contained, accessible, and contain no raster, gradient, or
external resource.
